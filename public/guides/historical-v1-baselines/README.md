# MAGNIOM Historical v1 Regulatory Baseline Specifications

> **Regulatory Traceability Archive (IEC 62304:2006+AMD1:2015 §5.8 / ISO 13485:2016 §4.2.4)**

This directory archives the historical **v1.0 foundation baseline specifications** of the MAGNIOM project.

---

## Regulatory Purpose

Under **IEC 62304 §5.8 (Software Release)** and **ISO 14971 (Risk Management)**, medical device software changes must preserve bi-directional traceability to the original design and requirements baseline under which historical clinical records were generated.

1. **Historical Immutability**:
   Historical v1 MDD target decision slates and clinical sign-off records generated during initial verification builds remain reproducible and reconstructable from their original scientific configuration.

2. **Superseded Status**:
   For all new multi-indication development, testing, and validation, the v1 specifications have been formally superseded by their **v2.0 counterparts** located in [`public/guides/`](file:///home/owner/Downloads/Magniom/public/guides):
   - [`MAGNIOM-Implementation & Validation Roadmap v1.0.md`](file:///home/owner/Downloads/Magniom/public/guides/historical-v1-baselines/MAGNIOM-Implementation%20&%20Validation%20Roadmap%20v1.0.md) is superseded by [`MAGNIOM-Implementation & Multi-Indication Validation Roadmap v2.0.md`](file:///home/owner/Downloads/Magniom/public/guides/MAGNIOM-Implementation%20&%20Multi-Indication%20Validation%20Roadmap%20v2.0.md).

3. **Complete v1 Suite**:
   The full suite of 15 original v1.0 specifications is also preserved in [`public/guides_v1/`](file:///home/owner/Downloads/Magniom/public/guides_v1).
