# Golden Case 08: Research Target Isolation & Ceiling Verification

## Purpose
Demonstrate strict mode gating where exploratory Tier 4 targets (Area 8Av) are blocked from clinical slates and permitted only in Research Mode.

## Expected Behaviour
- **Clinical Mode**: Area 8Av candidate is excluded by Gate 2 & Gate 5.
- **Research Mode**: Area 8Av candidate appears in exploratory position with prominent research warnings.
