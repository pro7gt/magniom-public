# Usability Engineering File (UEF) Template

**Standard Reference:** IEC 62366-1:2015 + AMD1:2020  
**Document Status:** Controlled Design-Control Template  

---

## 1. Intended User Profile & Use Environment
- **Primary User:** Specialist psychiatrists and clinical TMS neurophysiologists.
- **Use Environment:** Outpatient neuromodulation clinics and clinical research centers.

## 2. Safety-Critical User Interactions
- Formulating and confirming patient phenotype snapshot.
- Inspecting candidate target coordinates and evidence baselines.
- Reviewing counterfactual targets and contradictory evidence.
- Entering clinical rationale for target substitution or manual override.
- Final digital signing of treatment decision.

## 3. Automation Bias Safeguards
- Prohibit pre-selected candidate radio buttons or checkboxes.
- Require active clinician confirmation of uncertainty indicators and conflicting evidence.
