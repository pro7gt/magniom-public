## 2. Benefit-Risk Summary

All 20 identified hazards have been mitigated using the ISO 14971:2019 risk control hierarchy:
1. **Inherent Safety by Design:** Deterministic calculation core, strict geometry enums, indication-scoped evidence boundaries, and hard safety gating.
2. **Protective Measures in Software:** Automated QC filtering, coordinate transformation round-trip assertions, database RLS, and cryptographic release binding.
3. **Information for Safety:** No preselected default candidates, explicit CaseIndication routing, prominent evidence tier disclosures, and mandatory clinician prescriptive authority.

In all cases, the residual risk has been reduced to **Acceptable** (Remote or Improbable occurrence), confirming that the clinical benefits of personalised multi-indication neuromodulation targeting substantially outweigh the residual risks.
